Bulk Email Automation & Tracking Guide

1. Email Dumps & Campaign Archiving:

Every time you upload an Excel list and send an email, AutoMailer PRO saves the campaign name, original Excel sheet, recipient details, and message template into your local SQLite database (campaigns.db).

2. How "Seen vs Unseen" Tracking Works:

An invisible 1x1 tracking pixel is included in each outgoing HTML email. When your client opens the email in Gmail, Outlook, Apple Mail, or their smartphone, the server logs the exact open timestamp and marks that recipient as 👁️ SEEN.

3. Local vs Public URL for Tracking:

  • If running locally on your computer, enter your free Cloudflare Tunnel / ngrok URL in the "Tracking Base URL" field so external email opens reach your PC.
  • If deployed online (e.g. Render / Railway), the app auto-detects your public domain and tracking works 24/7!

4. Personalization & Anti-Spam:

  • Use {{Name}}, {{Company}} or any column header from your Excel sheet.
  • Keep the delivery delay at 2.5 - 3.0 seconds with the built-in natural jitter to avoid spam filters.
