Bulk Email Automation User Guide

1. Free Email Delivery & Sending Limits:

  • Personal Gmail: Up to 500 emails per day for free using a Google App Password.
  • Google Workspace / GSuite: Up to 2,000 emails per day.
  • Brevo (Sendinblue): 300 emails/day free with 99% inbox rate.
  • Custom Company SMTP: Unlimited (based on your company server limits).

2. How to Personalize Emails:

Your Excel spreadsheet can have any columns like Name, Company, Invoice, City. In your message, simply write {{Name}} or {{Company}} and the app automatically replaces it for each client!

3. Avoiding Spam Filters (Best Practices):

  • Keep the delay between emails at 2 - 3 seconds.
  • Use a legitimate "From Name" and clean subject lines.
  • Always test 1 email before sending to all 1,000 clients.
